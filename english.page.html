Experience

With 15 years of experience in Indian and Nepalese cuisine in Hiroshima, I've pursued unique flavors you can only find here, currently operating two locations. The COVID-19 pandemic has made the past few years very challenging for everyone. Our restaurant focuses on Ayurvedic cuisine, hoping to heal tired bodies and nurture self-healing abilities. With prior notice, we can accommodate halal and vegetarian requests - please don't hesitate to ask.

Challenging Ayurveda

Originating in India, Ayurveda emphasizes the body's natural healing abilities and focuses on restoring balance through diet. At Namaste, as an Indian-Nepalese restaurant, we aim to contribute to our customers' health by offering dishes made with dozens of spices and various vegetables. Please note our menu contains ingredients like eggs, dairy, wheat, soy, salad oil, cashews, coconut, and tomatoes - customers with allergies should take caution. For any menu questions, please ask our staff.
